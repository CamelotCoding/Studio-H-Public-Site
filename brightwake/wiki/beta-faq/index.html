Closed beta FAQ

Invites, wipes, costs and help.

← Back to Brightwake · Wiki home

Brightwake is in closed beta. Here is what that means for you.

How do I join?

The beta is invite-only. Sign up at brightwakegame.com. Invites are personal, so please don't sell or post invite codes.

Who can play?

Brightwake is for players aged 13 and over.

What does it cost?

Nothing. The beta is free, and Brightwake will stay free to play after launch.

Is there a cash shop?

After the beta, yes, and it sells looks only: glamour outfits, mounts, dyes and housing decor. Nothing in it makes you stronger. Every mount of a type moves at the same speed, whether it came from the shop or from play.

Will my progress be kept?

Not necessarily. This is a test, so things will break, and characters and items may be wiped during the beta.

Why can't my new account trade?

To protect everyone from bots and scams, trading, the Free Market and whispers to strangers unlock once your account is 7 days old, has 10 hours of play and has a verified email. Everyone has the same limits, supporters included.

How do I keep my account safe?

  • Verify your email and turn on two-factor login.
  • Never share your password. Staff will never ask for it.
  • Use the in-game report button for cheating, bots or abuse. You can also block players and filter chat.

How do I get help or report a bug?

Email support@brightwakegame.com. Tell us your world, your character name and what happened.
