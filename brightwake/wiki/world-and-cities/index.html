World and cities

Lumora, Saint Oriel, and the three cities she left behind.

← Back to Brightwake · Wiki home

Lumora is a world of three cities, held together by the memory of one saint and threatened by a fog that eats sound.

Lumora and Saint Oriel

Long ago a living fog called the Hush swallowed the old world. Saint Oriel, the Silent Saint, led the survivors out of it. Once they were safe, she walked back into the fog to hold it off, and the survivors went their separate ways and founded three cities. Each city keeps a part of her: her heart, her voice and her memory.

Vitrea

A seaside kingdom of stained glass, ruled by Queen Isolde. Light pours through coloured glass everywhere you look, and the city keeps Oriel's heart in a stained-glass shrine. People here speak in a courtly way.

  • Guilds: Squire and Priest.
  • City minigame: stained-glass puzzles.
  • Your mentor if you start here: a stained-glass keeper.

Brasswind

A desert city that runs on technology, not magic. Heat, steam and gears are everywhere. It is governed by an elected council of inventors led by Chief Engineer Tobin Vask, and Oriel's voice lives on inside a great machine, watched over by a clockwork statue of her.

  • Guild: Thief, plus the trainer for the Archer's Sharpshooter branch.
  • City minigame: gear races.
  • Your mentor if you start here: a clockwork engineer.

Solstara

An enchanted forest city ruled by the Court of Four Seasons: the Spring Heir, the Summer Queen, the Autumn King and the Winter Crone. Whichever monarch reigns follows the real-world season, and the city changes with them: its colours, music, quests, shops and what people say to you. Oriel's memory is kept in a sacred tree.

  • Guilds: Witch and Archer.
  • City minigames change with the season, such as snowball fights in winter and lantern catching in autumn.
  • Each season has its own festival with limited cosmetics that come back every year, and a festival boss that returns too.
  • Your mentor if you start here: a gardener of the Court.

Living cities

The cities are full of people with their own routines: children at play, guards on patrol, street musicians, gossips, quest givers and card players in the taverns. They react to the story, the time of day and the season.

The story so far

Levels 1 to 10
Life in your home city, and the first signs that the Hush is creeping closer.
Levels 10 to 30
Link the three cities by clearing the Hush from the roads between them.
Levels 30 to 50
Darker dungeons, and deeper questions about what the Hush really is.

You can finish the whole story solo: NPC companions join you where the story needs a group. Rumours speak of more of Lumora still hidden under the fog.
