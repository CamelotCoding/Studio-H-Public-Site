Getting started and controls

Your account, your first character, and how to play.

← Back to Brightwake · Wiki home

Everything you need for your first hour in Lumora: your account, your first character and the controls.

Your account

  1. Register with an email address, a username and a password, then confirm your email from the link we send you.
  2. Log in and pick a world. Each world has its own economy and its own players.
  3. Pick a channel. Channels are copies of the same world; you can switch channel at any time, for example to join friends.

An account has at least six character slots. Some things are shared by every character on the account: the monster journal, mounts, cosmetics, Flicker cards and housing.

Turn on two-factor login in your account settings. It is optional, but it is the best way to keep your characters safe.

Creating a character

Choose one of four races. Race changes how you look and how the world talks to you; it does not lock you out of any job.

Human
The most common folk in all three cities.
Elf
Long-lived and graceful.
Dwarf
Short, sturdy and stubborn.
Sereni
Descendants of the last survivors Saint Oriel led out of the Hush, changed by it. They have glowing freckle-like markings, pale eyes and hair that drifts as if underwater. Sereni can sense where the Hush is thin and make natural scouts, though some city folk distrust them.

Then shape your look: body type, skin tone, face, hairstyle and hair colour (and glow colour for Sereni), with several free choices of each. Every skin tone is free.

New characters start with a short animated intro and a tutorial in their home city, where a mentor shows them around.

Levels and experience

  • The level cap is 50.
  • At level 10 you choose a job; at level 30 you choose its branch.
  • Rested experience builds up while you are logged out, so short sessions still move you forward.
  • Dying costs a small slice of your experience, but never a level, and never inside dungeons or raids.
  • Gear wears down as you use it. Repair it for gold.

First-day tips

  • Your three beginner skills (Quick Step, Lantern Light and Second Wind) carry you to level 10. Lantern Light reveals hidden ledges and secret passages.
  • Sit down to recover health and your resource faster.
  • Open the monster journal (J) to see what drops where.
  • Read the beta FAQ for the rules on new accounts, trading and invites.
