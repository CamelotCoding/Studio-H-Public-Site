Skill lists

Every beginner, job and branch skill up to level 50.

← Back to Brightwake · Wiki home

Every skill you can learn up to level 50, by job and branch. Skills marked ★ are taught by your guild's class quest line; everything else is bought with skill points.

Each job has 6 active and 3 passive skills, and each branch adds 7 actives and 3 passives. By level 50 you choose 8 of your 13 actives for your skill bar, and all 6 passives are always on. Skill names may still change during the beta.

Beginner

  • Quick Step: a short dash.
  • Lantern Light: a glow that reveals hidden ledges and secret passages.
  • Second Wind (passive): recover faster while sitting.

Squire (Vitrea)

Squire

Resolve builds as you take and block hits.

  • Shield Bash: stuns an enemy
  • Shield Rush: charge forward and knock enemies back
  • Taunting Shout: draw enemies to you
  • Hold the Line ★: plant your shield and take less damage
  • Cleaving Swing: a wide arc
  • Rally Cry: allies take less damage

Passives: Iron Skin, Steady Footing, Stubborn Heart

Knight

The party's wall.

  • Bulwark Oath: a shield for the party
  • Radiant Guard ★: a glass dome that blocks projectiles
  • Challenge: a boss fights only you
  • Shield Throw: bounces between up to 5 enemies
  • Interpose: take the next hit meant for an ally
  • Holy Strike: turns Resolve into healing for yourself
  • Unyielding ★: half damage and no knockback

Passives: Guardian's Pride, Perfect Block, Vigilance

Berserker

The lower your health, the harder you hit.

  • Rending Spin
  • Last Stand ★: you can't drop below 1 HP
  • Bloodrush: spend health to fill Resolve
  • Ground Splitter: a shockwave
  • Reckless Leap
  • Rage Howl: faster attacks
  • Ruin ★: spend all your Resolve at once

Passives: Fury, Bloodthirst, Thick-Skinned

Priest (Vitrea)

Priest

Grace refills steadily.

  • Mending Light: heals the ally with the lowest health
  • Holy Bolt: extra damage to undead and Hush creatures
  • Sanctuary ★: a healing circle
  • Blessing: raises the party's max HP for a long time
  • Purify: clears harmful effects
  • Guiding Step: a short teleport

Passives: Devotion, Gentle Hands, Faithful

Cleric

Pure support.

  • Second Dawn ★: revive an ally
  • Lantern Ward: a shield for the party
  • Renewing Light: healing over time
  • Chorus of Light: a big area heal
  • Haste Prayer: faster movement and higher jumps
  • Rebuke: push enemies back
  • Dawnward ★: the party ignores knockback and status effects

Passives: Overflowing Grace, Mercy, Calm Presence

Shadow Priest

Curses that hurt enemies and heal you.

  • Penance Chains: bind an enemy and drain it
  • Eclipse ★: a shadow form where your hits heal you
  • Whisper of Doubt: enemies take more damage
  • Shadow Lance: a beam in a line
  • Siphon: drain health and share it with the party
  • Dread: small monsters flee
  • Night Litany ★: hits everything on screen

Passives: Communion, Lingering Pain, Dusk Mind

Witch (Solstara)

Witch

Mana; spells take on the look of Solstara's reigning season.

  • Spark: a bolt in the last element you used
  • Ember Wave: leaves the ground burning
  • Frost Lattice ★: an ice wall that slows enemies
  • Thunder Drop: strikes enemies above or below you
  • Blink: a short teleport
  • Mana Shell: damage is taken from your Mana instead

Passives: Arcane Mind, Spellweave, Seasoned Touch (each season boosts one element)

Sorcerer

Element combos: burning enemies explode under ice, and frozen enemies shatter under earth.

  • Stormlash: chain lightning
  • Elemental Confluence ★: all four elements at once
  • Meteor Shard
  • Glacier Spike
  • Inferno Pillar: reaches higher platforms
  • Earthen Wall
  • Elemental Attunement ★: every spell triggers a combo

Passives: Element Mastery, Chain Reaction, Mana Flow

Hexblade

A sword wreathed in spells.

  • Runecut: brands enemies
  • Spellsurge ★: your next 3 hits trigger runes
  • Arc Slash
  • Rune Leap
  • Hex Guard: parry a hit and return it as magic
  • Blade Storm
  • Sigil Burst ★: detonate every brand on screen

Passives: Runic Edge, Spellblade, Branding Mastery

Archer (Solstara)

Archer

Focus builds as you land hits from a distance.

  • Power Shot
  • Split Shot: three arrows at once
  • Windstep ★: a backflip with a gust of wind
  • Arrow Rain
  • Snare Arrow: roots an enemy in place
  • Keen Eye: raises critical hit chance

Passives: Bow Mastery, Light Feet, Patience

Bard

One song plays at a time.

  • Marching Song
  • Rallying Anthem ★
  • Lullaby Arrow: puts an enemy to sleep
  • Echo Shot
  • Ballad of Mending
  • Discord: confuses enemies
  • Encore ★: replay your last song

Passives: Resonance, Tempo, Harmony

Sharpshooter

Trained in Brasswind with technology bows and scopes.

  • Piercing Bolt
  • Deadeye ★: a charged shot
  • Scatter Charge
  • Grapple Shot: pull yourself to a far platform
  • Trip Mine
  • Mark Target
  • Overclock Scope ★: every shot is a critical hit for a while

Passives: Steady Aim, Long Range, Precision Engineering

Thief (Brasswind)

Thief

Energy refills fast; hits build combo points that finishers spend.

  • Double Stab
  • Shadowstep: teleport behind an enemy
  • Vanish ★: turn invisible and lose enemies' attention
  • Fan of Knives
  • Pilfer: sometimes steals coins or items
  • Smoke Bomb: enemies miss more

Passives: Nimble, Quick Hands, Opportunist

Assassin

Daggers and poison.

  • Venom Edge: stacking poison
  • Execute ★: a finisher
  • Ambush: strike from invisibility
  • Crippling Cut: slows an enemy
  • Twin Fang: a four-hit combo
  • Shadow Clone Strike
  • Death Mark ★: bursts for the damage it stored

Passives: Toxicology, Killer Instinct, Ruthless

Ninja

Throwing stars and clones.

  • Star Volley
  • Mirror Self ★: a clone copies your attacks
  • Kunai Chain: pulls an enemy to you
  • Wall Run
  • Shuriken Storm
  • Substitution: swap places with a log
  • Thousand Stars ★: fill the screen with stars

Passives: Star Mastery, Light Step (double jump), Shadow Bond
