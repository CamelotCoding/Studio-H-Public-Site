The Hush

The living fog that swallows sound.

← Back to Brightwake · Wiki home

The Hush is a living fog. It swallowed the old world, and it swallows sound.

What the Hush is

Nobody in the three cities remembers the world before the Hush. What they know is that it is alive, that it creeps, and that it brings silence. Saint Oriel walked back into it to hold it off, and it has been held at bay ever since. Now it is moving again.

Listen for it

You can hear the Hush before you see it. As you get close, a zone's music thins out, layer by layer, until only a few notes are left. Push the Hush back and the music returns.

Pushing it back

The world map starts mostly hidden under clouds, with one continent open. Exploring and clearing zones pushes the Hush back and reveals more of the map. The story's middle chapters are about clearing the roads between the three cities.

Creatures of the Hush

Near the fog the monsters change into twisted things. Priests' Holy Bolt does extra damage to undead and Hush creatures.

The Sereni

The Sereni are descended from the last survivors Oriel led out, and the Hush changed them. They can sense where it is thin, which makes them natural scouts.

Lanterns in the dark

In Lantern Climbs, jump puzzles set in darkness, your lantern lights only a small circle around you. Clearing one earns cosmetic rewards.
