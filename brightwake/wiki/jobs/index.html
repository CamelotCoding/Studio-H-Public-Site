Jobs and branches

Five jobs, ten branches, three guild cities.

← Back to Brightwake · Wiki home

Five jobs, each with two branches. Pick your job at level 10 and your branch at level 30.

How jobs work

  • Everyone plays as a beginner until level 10, then joins a job's guild.
  • At level 30 you choose one of your job's two branches.
  • Both choices are permanent for that character, so try a few characters if you are unsure. You have at least six slots.
  • Each job's guild has a class quest line that teaches its special skills, marked ★ on the skill lists.
  • Tanks and healers shine in boss fights and party quests, but every job has solid damage for playing solo.

Squire

The front line. Resolve builds as you take and block hits and is spent on strong skills.

  • Knight: the party's wall, with shields, taunts and protection for allies.
  • Berserker: Resolve turns to fury; the lower your health, the harder you hit.

Priest

The healer. Grace refills steadily, and bigger heals cost more of it.

  • Cleric: pure support, with revives, shields and big group heals.
  • Shadow Priest: faith turned inward, dealing damage with curses and healing itself as it fights.

Witch

The elemental caster, spending Mana. Witches' spells take on the look of Solstara's reigning season.

  • Sorcerer: pure elements and combos, such as burning enemies exploding under ice. Great at clearing crowds.
  • Hexblade: melee magic, with a sword wreathed in runes.

Archer

The ranged fighter. Focus builds as you land hits from a distance.

  • Bard: shoots and sings; one song at a time boosts or heals the party.
  • Sharpshooter: trained in Brasswind with technology bows and scopes, built for precision and boss damage.

Thief

Stealth and speed. Energy refills fast, and hits build combo points that finishers spend.

  • Assassin: daggers and poison for huge single-target damage.
  • Ninja: throwing stars, clones and mobility for many targets at once.

Skill names and numbers can still change during the beta.
