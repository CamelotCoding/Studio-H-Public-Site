Crafting and gathering

Seven crafting jobs and three gathering jobs.

← Back to Brightwake · Wiki home

Crafting and gathering are separate jobs on the same character, so your fighter can also be a master cook.

How it works

  • You can switch between crafting and gathering jobs freely; each levels up on its own.
  • Every craft is a quick one-screen minigame. Play it well for a high-quality result instead of a normal one.
  • Crafted gear and materials trade freely on the Free Market.

Crafting jobs

JobMakes
BlacksmithMetal weapons and armour
LeatherworkerLeather gear, from hides that drop from monsters
WeaverCloth gear
CarpenterWooden items
JewelerJewellery
CookFood that gives buffs
EnchanterEnchants for gear (see enchanting)

Gathering jobs

JobGathers
MinerOre and stone
HerbalistPlants and herbs

Finding materials

  • What you can gather depends on both your gatherer level and the area's level.
  • The best materials come from late-game areas.
  • Some materials, like hides, drop from monsters rather than being gathered.
