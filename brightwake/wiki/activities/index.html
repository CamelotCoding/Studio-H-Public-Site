Activities

Parties, dungeons, markets, mounts, housing and cards.

← Back to Brightwake · Wiki home

There is a lot more to do in Lumora than fight. Here is what you can get up to, alone or with friends.

Parties, guilds and friends

Form parties, take on party quests, join a guild, chat, keep a friends list and trade with other players. Party members follow their leader between channels.

Dungeons and the raid

  • Ten dungeons for parties of four, and one raid for eight players at level 50.
  • The group finder fills your party. Tanks and healers get a bonus for joining, but no strict lineup is needed.
  • A daily roulette, level sync and catch-up gear help everyone keep up.
  • Rather play alone? The main story can be finished solo with NPC companions.

The Free Market and trading

  • Each city has a Free Market with player stalls and a searchable board that shows price history. Sales pay a small tax.
  • Every world has its own economy.
  • Crafted gear, materials and Flicker cards trade freely. Boss gear and boss mounts are bound to you.
  • Brand-new accounts must wait before they can trade; see the beta FAQ.

Mounts

  • Your first ground mount comes at around level 20.
  • Flying unlocks after the main story, zone by zone, and only in zones you have cleared of the Hush.
  • Mounts work in the fields and on the world map, not in towns or dungeons.
  • Every mount of the same type has the same speed, so pick the one you like the look of.

Housing

Decorate an instanced room or a plot in a residential ward on a simple grid. Guilds can build guild halls with shared storage and buffs. Housing also has its own board games to play with friends.

Flicker, the card game

Flicker is played in taverns all over Lumora, and matches take about five minutes.

  • Play on a 4×4 board. Each card has four edge numbers and a light value.
  • Some squares are Hush squares that change the rules of play.
  • Each city has a house rule: in Vitrea captures reflect off glass, in Brasswind captures chain like gears, and in Solstara cards change with the season.
  • Cards drop from monsters. Challenge NPCs in taverns, enter tournaments or climb the ranked ladder.
  • Cards can be staked only in friendly matches.

Minigames and side challenges

  • Lantern Climbs: jump puzzles in the dark, lit only by your lantern, with cosmetic rewards.
  • City minigames: stained-glass puzzles in Vitrea, gear races in Brasswind and seasonal games in Solstara.
  • Party arena: the only player-versus-player fighting, just for fun. There is no open-world PvP.
