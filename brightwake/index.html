Brightwake

An original 2D pixel MMORPG

A bright, side-scrolling world to grind and adventure in with friends. Long ago a living fog called the Hush swallowed the old world. Now it is creeping back, and every zone you clear pushes it a little further away.

  • Closed beta, invite only
  • Free to play
  • Cash shop sells looks only
  • Controller or keyboard and mouse
  • Players 13+
Request an invite Read the Wiki Closed beta: sign-up is by invite at brightwakegame.com.

What you'll do

A world to share with friends

Three living cities

Vitrea, a seaside kingdom of stained glass and light. Brasswind, a desert city of heat, steam and machines. Solstara, an enchanted forest whose palette, music and festivals change with the real-world season.

Push back the Hush

The world map starts hidden under the Hush, a fog that swallows sound. Explore and clear zones to drive it back and bring their music home.

Pick your job

Choose Squire, Priest, Witch, Archer or Thief at level 10, then a branch at level 30, such as Cleric or Shadow Priest, Sorcerer or Hexblade. Each job has a guild in its home city with its own class quests.

Better together

Form parties, add friends and switch channels at any time inside your world. Tanks and healers shine in boss fights, but every job has solid solo damage. The level cap is 50 at launch.

Play your way

Full controller support with Xbox and PlayStation layouts, as well as keyboard and mouse, and you can remap your keys. On a controller, hold a trigger and press a face button to fire any of your eight skills.

Fair, never pay-to-win

The whole game is free. The cash shop sells looks only, such as outfits, dyes and mount skins. Nothing you can buy makes you stronger, and looks never affect your stats.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How do I join the beta?

Brightwake is in a closed beta, and sign-up is by invite only. Request an invite at brightwakegame.com.

How much does it cost?

Brightwake is free to play. The cash shop sells looks only, such as outfits, dyes, mount skins and housing decor. It never sells power, experience or early access to content.

What age is it for?

Brightwake is for players aged 13 and over. It's an online game with chat, so there's a chat filter, block lists and a report button, and brand-new accounts can't trade or message strangers until they've played for a while. Please read the Code of Conduct.

Can I use a controller?

Yes. Every screen and action works with a gamepad, with Xbox and PlayStation button layouts, and with keyboard and mouse. You can remap the keys too.

Can I play with my friends?

Yes, that's what Brightwake is for. Add friends, form a party and switch channels to meet up in the same world. Each account has at least six character slots.

Where can I learn more about the game?

The Brightwake Wiki covers the world, the jobs and how to play.
