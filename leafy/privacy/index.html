Leafy Tycoon: Idle Plant Shop: Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026 · Studio H

The short version. Leafy Tycoon is an idle plant shop game for players of all ages. There is no account and no sign-in. Your shop is saved on your device, and we do not collect personal data. The game has no analytics, no tracking and no third-party advertising in this version. Reminders are scheduled on your device. A bug report leaves your device only if you copy it and send it to us yourself. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google.

1. Who we are

Leafy Tycoon: Idle Plant Shop ("Leafy Tycoon", "the game") is made by Studio H, a DBA of House Heath LLC ("Studio H", "we", "us"). Studio H is responsible for the information described here. Contact us at developer@studiohgames.com.

2. Information the game handles

2.1 Stored only on your device

  • Game progress: your shop, plants, Plantdex, upgrades, gems, cuttings, achievements and settings (such as language, sound and reminders). It is saved on your device and is not sent to us.
  • Backup codes: Settings → Backup & transfer can turn your save into a code that you copy yourself, for example to move your shop to a new device. The code contains only your game progress. Where you keep or paste it is up to you; we never receive it.
  • Reminders: if you turn on Gentle reminders, the game schedules notifications on your device, such as "a daily gift is waiting" or "your greenhouse is overflowing". iOS asks for your permission first. No push server is involved, and nothing about the reminders is sent to us.
  • Bug reports: when you use Settings → Report a bug, the report is saved on your device (the last 20 at most). See section 2.2.

2.2 Bug reports you choose to send

A bug report contains the category you pick and what you write, the game version and build, your platform, screen size and browser engine (user agent) string, which tells us your device type and operating system version, a short summary of your shop progress (for example bench levels, coins, gems and minutes played), and any recent error messages from the game. It does not contain your name, email, contacts, location or any advertising identifier.

The game does not send the report anywhere by itself. You tap Copy report and paste it into an email to us or into TestFlight beta feedback, if you want to. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and we use them only to reply and fix the problem.

2.3 Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment card or billing details. The game receives a confirmation of the purchase so it can deliver your item, and it can restore purchases through the store. Your purchase history is governed by Apple's or Google's own privacy policy.

2.4 Ads

Ads in Leafy Tycoon are always optional: you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game bonus. This version of the game does not include a third-party ad network, so no information is collected for advertising and the game never asks for permission to track you. If we add an ad partner in a future update, we will update this policy before that update is released, and the game's Settings screen will say so.

2.5 What we do not collect

Leafy Tycoon has no account, no sign-in, no chat, no friends list and no other players. It does not use analytics or crash-reporting services, does not use the advertising identifier, and does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

3. How we use information

Information on your device is used only to run the game. Bug reports and emails you send us are used only to provide support, fix problems and improve the game. We do not sell personal information, we do not share it for advertising, and we do not use it to build a profile of you.

Legal bases (EU/UK): performance of our agreement with you (running the game and answering support requests) and legitimate interests (fixing bugs and improving the game).

4. Sharing

We do not share information from the game with anyone, because the game does not send us any on its own. Apple or Google process purchases through their stores. If you send a bug report through TestFlight, Apple delivers it to us under Apple's terms. We share information only when the law requires, or to protect the safety of players. If Studio H is ever involved in a merger or sale, this policy will continue to apply to your information.

5. Children

Leafy Tycoon is suitable for all ages. Because the game has no accounts, no chat and no analytics or ad tracking, it does not collect personal information from children. The only way information reaches us is if someone chooses to email us or send a bug report. If a parent or guardian believes a child has sent us personal information, email developer@studiohgames.com and we will delete it.

6. How long we keep information

  • On-device data stays until you delete it: use Settings → Reset game, or uninstall the game.
  • Support emails and bug reports you send us are kept only as long as we need them to help you and fix the problem, and then deleted.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Your save: export it, import it or erase it in Settings. Uninstalling the game also deletes it.
  • Reminders: turn off Gentle reminders in the game's settings, or turn off notifications for the game in your device's settings.
  • Bug reports: are always optional, and you can read the whole report before you copy it.
  • Access, correction, deletion, portability, objection: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, or US state laws such as the CCPA/CPRA), you may have these rights for anything you have sent us. Email us to use them; we will respond within the time the law requires. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.
  • California: we do not sell or share personal information.

8. International transfers

Studio H is based in the United States. If you email us or send a bug report from outside the United States, it is handled in the United States.

9. Security

Your game data stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect any information you send us.

10. Changes

We will post any changes here and update the effective date. If a change is significant, for example adding an ad partner, analytics or cloud saves, we will update this policy before the change reaches the game and tell you in the game.
