Leafy Tycoon

Idle Plant Shop

Inherit a tiny windowsill plant stand and grow it into a Grand Conservatory. Hire caretakers to keep the shop running while you're away, collect tropical plants and learn something true about every one.

What's inside

Grow a little shop into a big conservatory

Twelve plant benches

Start with a single Pothos and work up through 12 benches to the Bird of Paradise. Hire caretakers so every bench sells on its own.

Earns while you're away

Your caretakers keep the shop open while you're gone, for up to 8 hours at first and up to 24 hours with upgrades.

36 plants to collect

Open seed packs and Plant Hunts to fill your Plantdex with 24 rare plants from Uncommon to Legendary. Odds are published, with pity protection.

Real plant facts

Every plant has three fun facts and care tips, and visiting customers ask around 270 sourced trivia questions. There's a Weekly Trivia Challenge too.

New places to grow

Visit the Rainforest Expedition and the Desert Oasis, each with 12 plants of its own, and join the Bloom Festival with themed weeks like Carnivorous Plant Week.

Fair and relaxed

Customer orders have no deadlines, and missing a day never resets your daily gift. Ads are optional rewarded ads only, and pot skins are purely cosmetic.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Leafy Tycoon free?

Yes. Leafy Tycoon is free to download and play. There are optional in-app purchases, such as gem packs, pot skins and the Greenhouse Club, but you can grow the whole conservatory by playing.

Is it OK for kids?

Yes. Leafy Tycoon is made for all ages. There's no chat, no other players and no account to create. Plant facts are checked for accuracy, but please check care and pet-safety advice for your own plants with a trusted source such as the ASPCA.

Are there ads?

Only optional rewarded ads. You choose to watch one in exchange for a bonus, like doubling what your shop earned while you were away. There are no forced ads, ever.

Does my shop keep earning when I close the game?

Yes. Once you hire caretakers, they keep selling while you're away: up to 8 hours at first, and up to 24 hours with upgrades. Collect it all when you come back. If you turn on Gentle reminders in Settings, the game can let you know when your greenhouse is full.

How do I move my shop to a new device?

Your shop saves automatically on your device. To move it, open Settings → Backup & transfer, tap Export and copy the backup code. On the new device, paste the code into the same box and tap Import.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the Store and tap Restore purchases on the Gems tab. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID you used to buy. Subscriptions are managed in your App Store account settings.

How do I contact support?

Email developer@studiohgames.com. See the support section below for what to include so we can help quickly.
