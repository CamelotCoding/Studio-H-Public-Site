What you'll do

Hold the road against legend

Three civilizations

Lead the Hellenes, the Legions of Rome or the Two Lands of Egypt. Every tower, soldier, hero and spell changes with your choice, and you can switch before any battle.

120 towers from myth

Build Ranged, Barracks, Magic and Artillery towers, unlock a second line of each, then specialise into the Sanctuary of Artemis, the Temple of Janus, the Obelisk of the Sun and many more.

Twelve free heroes

Command Heracles, Achilles, Perseus, Romulus, Camilla, Horus, Sobek, Thoth and more. Each hero levels up as you play, and every one of them is earned in play.

Monsters of legend

Face 18 kinds of foe, from dragon-tooth warriors to tower-petrifying gorgons, and boss battles against the Minotaur, the Serpent of Chaos and Typhon.

Hours of challenge

24 battles across Greece, Egypt and Italy, three difficulties, Heroic and Iron trials, endless sieges, a daily challenge, the weekly Labor of Heracles and festivals from the real ancient calendar.

Frequently asked questions

Is Aegis of the Ancients free?

Yes. The full campaign of 24 battles across Greece, Egypt and Italy is free, and so are all twelve original heroes. Optional purchases include two expansions (Atlantis and the Northern Saga), Remove Ads, extra legendary heroes and skins. Nothing for sale makes you stronger through stars, upgrades or power.

Are there ads?

Only optional rewarded ads. The game never shows an ad on its own: you choose to watch one for a small bonus, such as a blessing before battle or an extra chest. If you buy Remove Ads, you keep those bonuses without watching anything.

Is it OK for kids?

It's a strategy game about heroes and monsters from mythology, so there is cartoon fantasy combat, but no blood and no chat between players. We expect it to suit players around 9 and up. Purchases go through Apple, so you can use Screen Time to require approval for them.

Can I play offline?

Yes. The whole game, including the daily, weekly and festival challenges, plays without a connection. Your progress is saved on your device. An internet connection is only needed for optional rewarded ads and for purchases.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases (you'll also find it in the Store). Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID you used to buy.

Which languages does it support?

English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German and Japanese.

How do I contact support?

Email developer@studiohgames.com. See the support section below for what to include so we can help quickly.
