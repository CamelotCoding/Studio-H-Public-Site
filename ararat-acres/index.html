Ararat Acres

After the Flood

The ark has come to rest on the mountains of Ararat. Help Noah's family plant the first fields of the new world, raise the animals that came off the ark, and turn a muddy flood plain into a thriving farm village.

What you'll do

A new world to farm

Fields and orchards

Grow 10 crops of the ancient Near East, from emmer wheat and lentils to melons and garlic, and tend olive, fig, pomegranate, date palm and grapevine. Drag across your fields to harvest or sow.

Animals from the ark

Raise doves, goats, sheep, cattle and bees, each with its own pen and feed, and collect what they give.

Workshops

Ten workshops and 27 recipes turn your harvest into flour, flatbread, lentil stew, olive oil, goat cheese, linen, pottery and hummus.

A story from Genesis

Fifteen quests over seven chapters follow Genesis 8–10, told by Noah, his wife, Shem, Ham, Japheth and the Little Dove. All dialogue is paraphrased and shows its scripture reference.

A busy farm

Fill traveller orders, set your own prices at the market stall, clear flood debris to make room, upgrade the granary and storehouse, and collect the wild pairs that visit two by two in the Ark Album.

Fair play

No energy system and no paid loot boxes. Your farm keeps growing while the app is closed, there are no banner ads, and no ads at all during the tutorial.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Ararat Acres free?

Yes. Ararat Acres is free to download and play. There are optional in-app purchases, such as gems, coins, a starter pack and the Covenant Pass, but there is no energy system and no paid loot boxes.

Is it OK for kids?

Yes. The game has no violence and nothing made by other players. The first time you play, it asks for a year of birth. Players under 13 get Kids Mode: no ads, no tracking prompt, and a grown-up check before any purchase. Leaving Kids Mode also needs the grown-up check. You can turn Kids Mode on at any time in Settings.

Are there ads?

Most ads are optional reward videos that you choose to watch, for example for free gems, a quicker harvest or double coins on an order. A few short ads may also appear at a natural break, after you close the order board. These never show before level 4, during the tutorial, more often than every 4 minutes, or in Kids Mode. There are no banner ads. The Supporter: No Ads purchase or the Shepherd's Club removes the break-time ads; reward videos stay available if you want them.

Is the story from the Bible?

The story is inspired by Genesis 6–10. All of the dialogue is our own paraphrase, and each line shows the scripture reference it comes from. The story ends at Noah planting his vineyard.

Where is my farm saved?

Your farm is saved on your device. There is no account and no cloud save, so deleting the game deletes your farm. Your purchases can always be restored from the store.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the Store (from the gem counter or Settings → Store) and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy. Shepherd's Club subscriptions are managed in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

How do I contact support?

Email developer@studiohgames.com. See the support section below for what to include so we can help quickly.
