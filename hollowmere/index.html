Hollowmere

Cozy Witch Farm

Welcome to Hollowmere, a sleepy valley where Hazel, a young witch, brings an old, overgrown farm back to life. Plant enchanted crops, brew potions, care for your animals and open a little apothecary for the townsfolk, all at your own pace.

  • iPhone and iPad
  • All ages
  • Free to play
  • No energy bar
Nov 6 on theApp Store Launching on the App Store Friday, November 6, 2026. Free to play on iPhone and iPad.

What you'll do

A farm full of magic

Grow a magical farm

Plant 25 enchanted crops, from quick Moonpetals and Glowcaps to rare crops that take a full day to grow. Place plots, trees and flowers anywhere, and unlock 8 large farm expansions.

Brew, craft and run your shop

Turn your harvest into potions, preserves and charms at bubbling crafting stations, serve customers at your apothecary counter and fill town requests.

Explore the valley

Follow a 12-chapter story full of secrets and gentle magic, catch 27 kinds of fish with unlimited fishing, and dig through 8 floors of a glittering mine.

Make it yours

Decorate your farm with over 150 cozy pieces and dress Hazel in robes, hats and accessories from her wardrobe.

Animals, Album and achievements

Look after Leili, your farm cat, and raise happy animals. Complete collections in your Album and earn Game Center achievements.

Visit friends

Visit other witches' farms with a friend code, send gifts, leave likes and stamp their guestbooks. Cloud save and Sign in with Apple keep your farm safe.

Seasonal events

Limited-time festivals bring event crops, mini-games and special rewards. The Harvest Feast runs November 19 to 30: bake pies, grow Heirloom Cranberries and play Falling Leaves.

Cozy and fair

No energy bar and crops never wither. Ads are optional rewarded ads only, and purchases are cosmetic or convenience. Nothing in the story is locked behind a payment.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Hollowmere free?

Yes. Hollowmere is free to download and play. Optional in-app purchases are cosmetic or for convenience, and nothing in the story is locked behind a payment.

When does Hollowmere come out?

Hollowmere launches on the App Store on Friday, November 6, 2026, free to play on iPhone and iPad. An Android version is planned for later.

What is Coven Membership?

An optional subscription ($6.99 per month or $49.99 per year; prices may vary by country). Members get 10 moonstones a day, an extra counter order slot, an extra queue slot at every station, a monthly members-only outfit piece, a member badge and rewarded-ad bonuses without watching ads. It renews automatically until you cancel, and you can manage or cancel it anytime in your App Store account settings.

Do I need to be online or sign in?

No. Hollowmere is fully playable offline with no sign-in, and your farm keeps growing while you are away. Go online to visit friends, and use Sign in with Apple if you want cloud save across devices.

Is it OK for kids?

Yes. Hollowmere is made for all ages, with no violence and no chat between players. The game asks for a birth year the first time you play. Players under 13 get only non-personalised ads, choose farm names from a list of approved words, and a grown-up must pass a parent check before any purchase or outside link.

Are there ads?

Only optional rewarded ads. You choose to watch one in exchange for a small in-game bonus. There are no banner ads and no ads that interrupt play.

How do I delete my account?

In the game, go to Settings → Account → Delete account. Tap the button twice to confirm. This deletes your online profile, friend code, farm picture, friends, gifts, likes and cloud save, then your account. Your farm stays on your device. You can also email developer@studiohgames.com with your friend code and we will delete it for you.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases (it is also at the bottom of the Boutique). Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID you used to buy. Subscriptions are managed in your App Store account settings.

How do I contact support?

Email developer@studiohgames.com. See the support section below for what to include so we can help quickly.
