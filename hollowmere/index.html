Hollowmere

Cozy Witch Farm

Play Hazel, a young witch who restores an overgrown farm on the edge of a little village. Grow magical crops, care for animals, brew potions and make the whole valley your own.

  • iPhone and iPad
  • All ages
  • Free to play
  • Android later

What you'll do

A farm full of magic

Magical farming

Grow 25 magical crops, from Moonpetal and Mandrake to Glowcap, with Silver and Gold quality harvests, sprinklers and fertiliser.

Animals and crafting

Keep hens, goats, bees, ducks, sheep and an owl. Brew and cook at the cauldron, oven, kitchen and workbench.

Places and story

Fish 27 kinds of fish, mine glittering crystals, serve town orders and follow a 12-chapter story about the valley.

Decorate everything

Rearrange the whole farm: plots, trees, paths, fences, stations and decorations. Choose seasonal themes and a wardrobe for Hazel.

Friends

Visit friends' farms by code, send likes and daily gifts, and join weekly community goals. Everyone starts with the computer friend Morwen.

Fair play

No energy bars and crops never wither. Ads are optional rewarded ads only, and purchases are cosmetic or convenience.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Hollowmere free?

Yes. Hollowmere is free to download and play. Optional in-app purchases are cosmetic or for convenience, and everything on the farm can be earned by playing.

Is it OK for kids?

Yes. Hollowmere is made for all ages, with no violence and no chat between players. The game asks for a birth year the first time you play. Players under 13 get only non-personalised ads, choose farm names from a list of approved words, and a grown-up must pass a parent check before any purchase or outside link.

Are there ads?

Only optional rewarded ads. You choose to watch one in exchange for a small in-game bonus. There are no banner ads and no ads that interrupt play.

How do I delete my account?

In the game, go to Settings → Account → Delete account. This deletes your online account and its data (profile, farm snapshot, friends, gifts and cloud save). You can also email developer@studiohgames.com with your friend code and we will delete it for you.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases (it is also at the bottom of the Boutique). Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID you used to buy. Subscriptions are managed in your App Store account settings.

How do I contact support?

Email developer@studiohgames.com. See the support section below for what to include so we can help quickly.
