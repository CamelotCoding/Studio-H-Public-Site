Life in the valley

A little magic every day

Tend the fields by moonlight, stir something sweet in the cauldron and see what the valley has in store next.

Grow enchanted crops

Plant 25 magical crops, from quick Moonpetals and Glowcaps to rare ones that take a full day. Your farm keeps growing while you're away.

Brew and run your apothecary

Turn your harvest into potions, preserves and charms, then serve villagers at your own shop counter and fill town requests.

Uncover a 12-chapter story

Gentle secrets lead you into town, down to the water and deep underground. Catch 27 kinds of fish and dig through a glittering mine.

Decorate farm and cottage

Place plots, trees, lanterns and cozy pieces anywhere you like, furnish your cottage and dress Hazel in robes and hats.

A cat familiar by your side

Leili, your farm cat, keeps you company, and happy animals fill the meadow as your farm grows.

Visit friends' farms

Drop by other witches' farms, send gifts, leave likes and stamp their guestbooks.
