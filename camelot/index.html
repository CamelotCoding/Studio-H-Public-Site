Camelot Tea Legends

Idle Hero-Collector RPG

The court jesters Blueberry and Raspberry have betrayed King Arthur for Morgan le Fay. Gather the Squire, the Cleric and the heroes of the Camelot Tea Company blends, and set things right.

  • iPhone and iPad
  • Free to play

  • Android later

What you'll do

A kingdom steeped in legend

32 heroes to collect

Eight each of Common, Rare, Epic and Legendary, from the Squire and Merlin to Sir Lancelot and Nimue. Each has a basic attack and three skills unlocked by stars.

A town for a home

Every menu is a building in the pixel-art town of Camelot: the Wizard's Tower, the Hall of Heroes, the Tea House, the Library, the Treasury and more.

A 12-chapter story

Fight through 120 stages, from the Drowned Shore to the Bridge of Swords, then carry on into the Endless Quest. Merlin's Tower, jousts and festivals wait beyond the story.

The Blacksmith's forge

Give every hero up to five pieces of gear in five tiers, forge and enhance them, and watch each piece appear on your hero in battle.

Idle rewards

Your heroes keep questing while you're away. Gold and EXP bank in the Royal Treasury for up to 12 hours, and a daily calendar of gifts grows through the month.

Fair play

No banner ads and nothing that interrupts play. Ads are optional rewarded ads only, never more than six a day, and purchases never renew by themselves.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Camelot Tea Legends free?

Yes. It is free to download and play. Optional in-app purchases in the Royal Vault give gems and other extras. The Royal Tea Subscription and the season pass don't renew by themselves: nothing is charged again unless you choose to buy again.

Are there ads?

Only optional rewarded ads. You choose to watch one for a bonus, such as two hours of gold and EXP from the Royal Treasury, up to six a day. There are no banner ads and no ads that interrupt play. The Royal Seal gives you the ad rewards without watching ads.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. There are no accounts and no cloud saves. To move to a new phone or keep a safe copy, open Settings and copy your save code, then paste it in on the other device.

What are tea codes?

Some Camelot Tea Company orders come with a printed code card. Enter the code in Settings for an in-game gift. Each code can be used once per save.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases (it is also on the Merchant screen). Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID you used to buy. The Royal Seal is restored this way; gems and other items are used up when you receive them.

How do I report a bug?

Go to Settings → Report a bug. Your report is saved on your device. Open My reports, tap Copy, and paste it into an email to developer@studiohgames.com.
