What you'll do

One tap, one patch of colour

Tap to paint

Every tap fills in a cell. Watch a pixel-art picture appear square by square, from a little heart to sunsets, animals and faraway places.

Hire helpers

Apprentices, painters, muralists, art classes, paint robots and more keep painting for you, even while you are away for a little while.

Collect 2,000 pictures

Every finished picture goes into your gallery and raises your gallery bonus, so the next one paints faster.

Daily gifts

Come back each day for a gift that grows over a seven-day streak. Turn on reminders if you like; they are off until you say yes.

Upgrades and boosters

Upgrade your brush and train your helpers. Grab a bonus paint drop, double speed or an hour of coins when you want a boost.

Cozy and fair

No accounts and no sign-in. Your progress stays on your device. Share any finished picture with friends straight from your gallery.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Picture Patch free?

Yes. Picture Patch is free to download and play. Everything in the game can be earned by playing. The optional No-Ads Pass and tips are the only purchases.

Where can I play Picture Patch?

Picture Patch is coming soon to the App Store for iPhone and iPad, and to Google Play for Android.

Are there ads?

Yes, from Google AdMob. Rewarded ads are optional: you choose to watch one for a bonus paint drop, double speed or an hour of coins. Pop-up ads appear at most once every 7 minutes, at natural breaks. All ads are non-personalised, and the game never asks to track you.

What does the No-Ads Pass do?

It is a one-time purchase that removes pop-up ads and makes boosters instant, with no ad to watch. Tips are optional ways to support the game and unlock nothing.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the Shop tab and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy.

Is my progress saved online?

No. There are no accounts, and your progress is saved only on your device. Deleting the game deletes your progress, but you can restore the No-Ads Pass from the store.

Is it OK for kids?

Picture Patch is made for all ages. There is no chat, no sign-in and no way to post anything. All ads are non-personalised. A grown-up may want to keep an eye on purchases, which go through Apple or Google.

How do I turn reminders on or off?

Open the Gallery tab and use the reminders switch, or change notifications for Picture Patch in your device settings.

How do I contact support?

Email developer@studiohgames.com. See the support section below for what to include so we can help quickly.
