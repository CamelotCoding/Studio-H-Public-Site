Picture Patch: Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026 · Studio H
Updated November 8, 2026: players aged 16 and over can now get personalised ads (with tracking permission on iPhone and iPad), and the game asks your birth year once to choose the right ads.

The short version. Picture Patch is a cozy tapping game for players of all ages. Your progress is saved on your device only. We have no accounts, no servers that hold your data, and no analytics of our own. The game shows ads from Google AdMob. It asks your birth year once, and the answer stays on your device. Players 16 and over can get personalised ads (on iPhone and iPad only if they allow tracking). Players under 16, or who don't answer, get non-personalised ads suitable for all ages and are never asked to allow tracking. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We do not sell your personal information. Uninstalling the game removes everything it stored.

1. Who we are

Picture Patch ("the game") is made by Studio H, a DBA of House Heath LLC ("Studio H", "we", "us"), based in Colorado, United States. Studio H is responsible for the information described here. Contact us at developer@studiohgames.com.

2. Information we handle

2.1 Stored only on your device

  • Game progress: your pictures, coins, helpers, upgrades, gallery, daily gift streak and settings. It is saved on your device only and is never sent to us. We cannot see it or restore it.
  • Reminders: if you say yes when the game asks, it schedules local notifications on your device (for example when your helpers are full or a daily gift is ready). No push server is involved, and nothing is sent to us. You can turn reminders off in the game's Gallery tab or in your device's settings.
  • Shared pictures: when you choose to share a picture, the game makes an image on your device and hands it to your device's share sheet. Where it goes next is up to you and the app you pick. We do not receive it.

2.2 Purchases

In-app purchases (the No-Ads Pass and optional tips) are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). They handle your payment. We never see your payment card, billing address, Apple ID or Google account. The store tells the game which product was bought, and the game keeps that on your device so it can unlock the No-Ads Pass and restore it later. We do not keep a copy on any server.

2.3 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform (consent) SDK. There are optional rewarded ads (you choose to watch one for a bonus paint drop, double speed, an hour of coins, or doubling what your helpers earned while you were away) and pop-up ads shown at most once every 7 minutes at natural breaks. The No-Ads Pass removes the pop-up ads.

Age question. After you finish your first picture, the game asks your birth year once, so it can choose the right kind of ads. The answer is stored only on your device and is never sent to us. Google receives only the resulting ad settings (for example "treat as a child"), not your birth year. You can change the answer at any time with the "Age · change" link in the game's Gallery tab. To be safe, the game counts your age as the youngest you could be this year (for example, someone born in 2010 counts as 15 throughout 2026).

Players 16 and over can get personalised ads, chosen partly from your activity in this and other apps, and limited to content rated PG or lower:

  • Where the law requires consent (for example in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland), ads are personalised only if you agree in Google's consent form (section 2.4).
  • On iPhone and iPad, the game first shows a short note and then Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. Only if you allow tracking can Google use the advertising identifier (IDFA) for personalised ads. If you ask the app not to track, Google shows ads without the IDFA.
  • On Android, Google may use the advertising ID (AAID) for personalised ads, following your device's ad settings.

Players aged 13 to 15 get only non-personalised ads suitable for all ages (G-rated). Their ad requests are tagged as from a user under the age of consent, and the game never shows them the tracking prompt.

Players under 13, or who don't answer the age question, get only non-personalised ads suitable for all ages (G-rated). Their ad requests are tagged as directed to children and as from a user under the age of consent, and the game never shows them the tracking prompt.

For every player, to show, measure and protect ads, Google may process:

  • Device identifiers: on Android, the advertising ID (AAID) and app set ID, which you can reset or delete in your device settings; on iOS, the IDFA only if you allow tracking, otherwise only identifiers Apple allows without tracking permission;
  • IP address, and an approximate location worked out from it (city or region level, not GPS);
  • Ad interactions: which ads were shown, taps, video views and app launches;
  • Crash and diagnostic data from the SDK, such as crash logs, app launch time and performance.

Google uses this for advertising (including personalised advertising for players 16 and over who allow it), ad measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. Non-personalised ads are chosen from context (such as the app and your general location), not from a profile of you. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's personalised and non-personalised ads.

We only work with service providers, such as Google AdMob, whose own privacy policies provide the same or equal protection of your data as described in this policy.

You can report an inappropriate ad by emailing developer@studiohgames.com or through the ad's AdChoices icon.

2.4 Consent form

Where the law requires it (for example in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland), the game shows Google's consent form the first time it loads ads, so you can choose how Google may use data for ads. If you don't agree to personalised ads, or you are under 16, you get non-personalised ads.

3. How we use information

The game uses the information on your device only to run the game: keep your progress, unlock what you bought, and send the reminders you asked for. Ad data is processed by Google as described in section 2.3. We do not sell personal information, we do not build profiles, and we do not run analytics of our own.

Legal bases (EU/UK): performance of our agreement with you (running the game), legitimate interests (showing non-personalised ads to keep the game free, and preventing ad fraud), and consent where the law requires it (personalised ads, through the consent form described in section 2.4, which you can withdraw at any time).

4. Sharing

The game shares information only with Google (AdMob, for ads) and with Apple or Google for purchases and the app store. We also share information when the law requires it. Studio H itself does not receive or store your personal information through the game. If you email us, we use your message and email address only to answer you.

5. Children

Picture Patch is suitable for all ages. It has no accounts, no chat, no messages between players and no way to post content. The game itself does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. We do not knowingly let personalised ads reach players under 16. Players aged 13 to 15 get only non-personalised, G-rated ads, tagged as from a user under the age of consent, and are never asked for tracking permission. When a player gives a birth year that makes them under 13, or doesn't answer the age question, their ads switch to non-personalised, G-rated ads, tagged as directed to children and as from a user under the age of consent, and the game never asks them for tracking permission. The only data processed then is what the Google Mobile Ads SDK needs to show, measure and protect ads (section 2.3). The birth year stays on the device and is never sent to us. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

6. How long we keep information

  • Game progress, purchase records and reminder settings stay on your device until you reset the game (Gallery → Reset progress) or uninstall it.
  • Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer you, and for no more than 12 months.
  • Google keeps ad data under its own retention policies; see how Google retains data.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your data: uninstalling the game removes everything it stored on your device. "Reset progress" in the Gallery tab clears your progress (purchases can be restored from the store).
  • Ads: rewarded ads are always optional, and the No-Ads Pass removes pop-up ads. To stop personalised ads: on iPhone and iPad, turn off tracking for the game in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; on Android, go to Settings → Google → Ads to reset or delete your advertising ID. You can change your age answer with the "Age · change" link in the game's Gallery tab. Where the consent form is shown, you can change your choice later through the form when it is offered.
  • Reminders: turn them off in the Gallery tab or in your device's notification settings.
  • Access, correction, deletion, portability, objection: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, or US state laws such as the CCPA/CPRA), you may have these rights. We do not hold personal information about you on our side, but email us and we will help, including with requests about ad data processed by Google. We will respond within the time the law requires. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.
  • California: we do not sell personal information. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16. Personalised ads for players 16 and over may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising under California law; you can opt out at any time by turning off tracking (iPhone and iPad) or deleting your advertising ID (Android) as described above. Players under 16 get only non-personalised ads.

8. International transfers

Studio H is based in the United States. Google may process ad data in the United States and other countries, using safeguards recognised by law, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

9. Security

Your progress stays on your device. Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep what we handle to a minimum.

10. Changes

We will post any changes here and update the effective date. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the game.
